Senior Principal Writer at Insomniac Games

I write interactive stories built around character. At Insomniac I wrote on Marvel's Wolverine and Marvel's Spider-Man 2, and was a lead writer on Ratchet & Clank: Rift Apart. Before that, I wrote episodes of The Walking Dead: The Final Season and Batman: The Enemy Within at Telltale Games. I'm currently working on an unannounced project.
